# Hi, I'm Christoph 👋

**I lead. I consult. I code.** All three around one topic: AI transformation, on the job and in my own life. Based in Zurich.

My view: AI transformation isn't about adding a chatbot on top. It changes how the work gets done, so the way people work has to change with it. I test that on my own work first and write down what happened, including what didn't work.

### On the job

- **I lead** a Data & AI team, and we work AI-first: agents and automation in development, research, architecture and sales, with people keeping the responsibility.
- **I consult** leadership teams on how AI changes the way their business operates, and on the governance that makes it hold up: security, identity, regulation and cost.
- **I code.** Hands-on architecture and engineering on data and AI platforms: Microsoft Azure, Fabric, Databricks, Snowflake and Azure AI Foundry.

### In private

- **[Recompiled](https://chris-koenig.github.io)**, my blog in English and [German](https://chris-koenig.github.io/de/). Two agents I set up run it: one owns the writing, one owns the site. I review everything before it goes out.
- **A career-network manager built on Claude.** After a meeting I just talk into it. It keeps track of who I know, what we discussed and what I still owe someone. It never acts without my go.
- **Skills made from my own corrections.** Instead of explaining my preferences to AI again and again, I turn them into reusable skills.
- **A family assistant** that takes emails, appointments and paperwork off our plate at home.
